Questions

Good to know

Is Katuwang really free?

Yes. It is a non-profit school project — no payments, no premium tier.

Do I have to use my real name?

You register with it, but other students only ever see your anonymous ID.

Who can be a tutor?

Students who apply for a subject and pass a short quiz on each topic they want to teach.

What if I can’t find a class?

Post a topic request. A verified tutor can pick it up and schedule a class.
