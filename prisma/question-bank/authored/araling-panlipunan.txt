## Ekonomiks

Which is a production input?|Labor.|Finished consumption alone.|A brand slogan only.|A customer's receipt only.|Labor contributes to producing goods and services.
A firm sells goods for ₱500 with total cost ₱350. Profit?|₱150.|₱850.|₱350.|₱500.|Profit equals revenue minus cost.
Which best describes a budget?|A plan allocating income among uses.|A guarantee of unlimited spending.|Only a debt collector's notice.|A list of prices with no income information.|A budget coordinates available funds and planned spending.
Why can savings support future spending?|They set aside current resources for later use.|They guarantee every investment gains.|They eliminate every expense.|They always double immediately.|Saving transfers purchasing capacity across time.
A drought reduces harvest while demand stays unchanged. What market tendency is expected?|Lower supply and upward price pressure.|More supply and lower price necessarily.|No possible price change.|Demand automatically disappears.|A supply reduction can raise equilibrium price.
