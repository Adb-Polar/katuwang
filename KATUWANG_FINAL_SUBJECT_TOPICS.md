**Coverage is partial and version-specific; this is not a complete or DepEd-certified list.** Topics are curriculum domains, units, or specialization categories, not every learning competency. Junior-high entries reproduce the legacy LRMDS index; current revised K–10/MATATAG coverage is not established by that index. SHS entries below use inspected Strengthened SHS guides, with explicit Academic or Technical Professional tracks and clusters in the source report. Legacy 2016 SHS strand subjects are not silently mixed into these courses.

See [CURRICULUM_SOURCES.md](CURRICULUM_SOURCES.md) for verification levels, per-grade sources, elective scope, and missing coverage. The requested filename is retained; “FINAL” does not imply completeness or official endorsement.
