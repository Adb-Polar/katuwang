# Curriculum grade review

Reviewed 27 October 2026 for school year 2026–2027.

All 7 existing subjects and 43 existing topics now have grade coverage metadata. These are mappings of the app’s broad tutoring labels to curriculum content, not official DepEd course names or an exhaustive competency catalog. A grade assignment can cover only part of a combined label; notes identify these cases. This is a source-based desk review, not DepEd certification.

The primary cross-check is the [DepEd Learning Systems 2026 three-term Budgets of Work](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/lsguide/home), including Grades 7–10 documents and sector-specific TLE documents. Full 2023 MATATAG guides provide supporting detail and are available from the [DepEd resource portal](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/deped-lrportal/revised-k-10-lms). Page numbers below refer to the supporting full guide unless otherwise identified. Current BOW links accompany each entry.

Multiple grades are intentional. Whole-number arithmetic is foundation review. Translation (Pagsasalin) is supported by a legacy Grade 10 resource, but was not located as an explicit competency in the revised Filipino guide or current Grade 10 BOW; it remains flagged as legacy coverage, not verified current coverage. Do not use that tag as proof of a current required competency.

The seven categories are junior-high catalog categories. Grades 11–12 require separate SHS course/elective mappings; they are not inferred from these names. See [DepEd Strengthened SHS](https://www.deped.gov.ph/strengthened-shs-program/) and [DM 12, s. 2026](https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/DM_s2026_012r.pdf). MAPEH remains the app umbrella; source guides distinguish Music and Arts and PE and Health. Home Economics is mapped to Family and Consumer Science. TLE specializations depend on the school’s offerings.

Newer [2026 DepEd training notices](https://caraga.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RM-NO.-0305-S.-2026-CORRIGENDUM-TO-THE-REGIONAL-MEMORANDUM-0264-S.-2026-RE-CONDUCT-OF-THE-TRAINING-OF-CORE-AND-REGIONAL-TRAINERS-ON-THE-REVISED-GRADES-6-9-AND-10-CURRICULUM.pdf) include Grades 6, 9 and 10 in the final 2026–2027 rollout. The older 2024 schedule alone is insufficient for determining current Grade 10 coverage.

## Implementation and applying the data

The migration adds `gradeLevels` (JSON array of GradeLevel enum values) and `curriculum` (source evidence) to `subjects` and `topics`, and backfills exact canonical names. Unrecognized custom entries remain unassigned rather than receiving guessed grades. Admin create/edit forms require nonempty, valid grade selections; custom/admin edits replace the original research attribution with an admin qualification. Public catalog responses retain `topics: string[]` for compatibility and add `gradeLevels`, `curriculum`, and `topicDetails`.

From the repository, apply the reviewed migration using the usual database deployment process (`prisma migrate deploy`). It includes the canonical data backfill; do not run the full demo seed. `tsx prisma/backfillCurriculum.ts` provides a dry-run for an already migrated database, and `tsx prisma/backfillCurriculum.ts --apply` reapplies the reviewed metadata to matching canonical rows. Both seed entrypoints also include the grade metadata for fresh installations.
