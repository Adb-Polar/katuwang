# Comprehensive Philippine K–12 Curriculum Research Report (Grades 7–12)

**Project:** Katuwang Peer Tutoring Platform  
**Target Institution:** Taysan Resettlement Integrated School (TRIS), Legazpi City, Albay, Philippines  
**Date of Research:** October 2026  
**Status:** Completed & Source-Verified against Official Department of Education (DepEd) Documents  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This research report establishes the official curriculum standards, subject taxonomies, content domains, topics, subtopics, and learning competencies for the Philippine basic education system across **Grades 7 to 12**. The findings serve as the foundation for the curriculum database of **Katuwang**, a peer tutoring management system designed to support students and student tutors at Taysan Resettlement Integrated School (TRIS).

Rather than relying on generic subject lists or informal outlines, this report synthesizes authoritative primary documentation from the **Department of Education (DepEd)**, including:
1. The **MATATAG Curriculum (DepEd Order No. 010, s. 2024)** and 2024–2026 Budget of Work (BoW) documents.
2. The **Enhanced Basic Education Curriculum (Republic Act 10533)**, 2016 Curriculum Guides, and **Most Essential Learning Competencies (MELCs, DepEd Order No. 012, s. 2020)**.
3. The **Senior High School Curriculum Guides** (Core, Applied, and Academic/TVL Specializations) and the **Strengthened Senior High School (SSHS)** curriculum transition frameworks.

All identified subjects, topics, and competencies have been cataloged with direct government URLs, official publication titles, grade placements, and traceability references.

## 2. Applicable Curriculum Versions & Policy Context

### 2.1 The MATATAG Curriculum (K to 10)
- **Legal & Administrative Basis:** DepEd Order No. 010, s. 2024 (*Policy Guidelines on the Implementation of the MATATAG Curriculum for the K to 10 Basic Education Program*) amending DepEd Order No. 021, s. 2019.
- **Rollout Schedule for Junior High School:**
  - **SY 2024–2025:** Grade 7 (Phase 1)
  - **SY 2025–2026:** Grade 8 (Phase 2)
  - **SY 2026–2027:** Grade 9 (Phase 3)
  - **SY 2027–2028:** Grade 10 (Full Key Stage 3 implementation)
- **Key Objectives:** Decongestion of learning competencies, focus on foundational literacy and numeracy, cognitive optimization, streamlined spiral progression, and institutionalization of Values Education under Republic Act No. 11476.

### 2.2 Traditional K to 12 Curriculum & MELCs (Transition Baseline)
- **Legal & Administrative Basis:** Republic Act No. 10533, DepEd Order No. 21, s. 2019, and DepEd Order No. 012, s. 2020 (*Adoption of the Most Essential Learning Competencies*).
- **Current Role:** Operates as the official standard for cohorts preceding MATATAG rollout (notably Grade 10 during the transition period) and provides historical continuity for diagnostic assessments and remedial tutoring.

### 2.3 Senior High School (Grades 11–12) Framework
- **Current Standard:** DepEd Order No. 021, s. 2019 / DepEd Order No. 031, s. 2020, governing the 15 Core Subjects, 7 Applied/Contextualized Subjects, and Track Specializations (Academic Track: STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS; Technical-Vocational-Livelihood Track: ICT, HE, IA, AFA).
- **Strengthened Senior High School (SSHS) Pilot:** DepEd 2024–2026 SSHS pilot featuring consolidated core domains (Effective Communication, General Mathematics, General Science, Life and Career Skills, Philippine History & Society) and cluster electives.

## 4. Junior High School Curriculum Structure (Grades 7–10)

Junior High School covers Key Stage 3 across eight major learning areas.

### 4.1 Mathematics
- **Grade 7:**
  - *Term 1 (Measurement & Number Sense):* Regular and irregular polygons, interior and exterior angle relationships, percentage increases/decreases, consumer money problems, financial planning, representation and operations on rational numbers, square roots of perfect squares, cube roots of perfect cubes, irrational numbers.
  - *Term 2 (Number and Algebra):* Algebraic expressions, terms, coefficients, variables, exponent laws, polynomial operations, solving linear equations and linear inequalities in one variable.
  - *Term 3 (Data & Probability):* Formulating statistical questions, sampling techniques, frequency distributions, histograms, measures of central tendency.
- **Grade 8:**
  - *Term 1 (Data & Algebra):* Measures of central tendency for ungrouped and grouped data, polynomial operations (monomials, binomials, multinomials), special products, factoring polynomials (common monomial factor, difference of two squares, sum/difference of cubes, trinomials), rational algebraic expressions and equations, arithmetic and geometric sequences, Cartesian plane (plotting, distance, midpoint).
  - *Term 2 (Algebra & Functions):* Linear equations in two variables, slope, intercepts, graphing lines, systems of linear equations (graphical, substitution, elimination methods), linear inequalities in two variables.
  - *Term 3 (Geometry):* Deductive reasoning, axiomatic structure, triangle congruence postulates and theorems (SAS, ASA, SSS, AAS), CPCTC, parallel lines cut by a transversal.
- **Grade 9:**
  - *Term 1 (Geometry & Relations):* Points, lines, planes, parallel and perpendicular constructions, angles formed by parallel lines cut by a transversal, quadrilaterals and parallelograms (rectangles, rhombi, squares), relations and functions, domain and range, slope and zeros of linear functions.
  - *Term 2 (Algebra & Radicals):* Quadratic equations (extracting roots, factoring, completing the square, quadratic formula), quadratic functions (vertex, axis of symmetry, domain, range, graphing), variations (direct, inverse, joint, combined), simplifying and operating with radical expressions and equations.
  - *Term 3 (Geometry & Trigonometry):* Triangle similarity theorems, right triangle trigonometry, trigonometric ratios (sine, cosine, tangent), angles of elevation and depression, solving oblique triangles.
- **Grade 10:**
  - *Term 1 (Trigonometry & Analytics):* Laws of Sines and Cosines (including ambiguous cases), geometric transformations (translations, reflections, rotations in coordinate planes), quadratic inequalities in one and two variables, absolute value equations and inequalities, measures of position (quartiles, deciles, percentiles, interquartile range, outliers, box-and-whisker plots).
  - *Term 2 (Sequences & Circles):* Arithmetic, geometric, harmonic, and Fibonacci sequences; polynomials and polynomial equations; geometric theorems on circles (chords, arcs, central/inscribed angles, tangents, secants).
  - *Term 3 (Combinatorics & Probability):* Fundamental counting principle, permutations and combinations, probability of compound, independent, and dependent events.

### 4.2 Science (Spiral Progression)
- **Grade 7:** Particle model of matter, changes of state, solutions, solubility, acid-base indicators, compound microscope operation, plant vs. animal cell cytology, biological organization, motion in one dimension, waves, sound, light, heat, Philippine geography, atmospheric phenomena (monsoons, ITCZ, breezes).
- **Grade 8:** Digestive, circulatory, and respiratory system coordination; Mendelian genetics and inheritance patterns; cellular energy (photosynthesis and cellular respiration); atomic structure and subatomic particles (protons, neutrons, electrons, isotopes); Newton's laws of motion, work, and power; earthquakes, active faults, and tropical cyclones.
- **Grade 9:** Newton's laws in two dimensions, projectile motion, impulse and momentum; electric circuits, Ohm's law, electrical safety; continental drift and lithospheric plate movements; quantum mechanical model of the atom, electron configurations, ionic and covalent chemical bonding; hydrocarbons and carbon chemistry.
- **Grade 10:** Chemical reaction types, collision theory, balancing chemical equations, reaction rates; nervous and endocrine system coordination, homeostasis, negative feedback; DNA replication, transcription, translation, mutations, and evolutionary lines of evidence; electromagnetic spectrum, light reflection, refraction, curved mirrors, lenses; plate tectonics and disaster mitigation.

### 4.3 English
- **Grade 7:** Philippine literature across pre-colonial, colonial, and contemporary eras; style and elements of poetry (lyric, narrative, dramatic) and prose; multiliteracies and multimodal publishing; expository, informational, and journalistic text analysis; active/passive voice, direct/reported speech.
- **Grade 8:** Afro-Asian literature reflecting diverse regional cultures; multimodal literacy; transition signals, modals, conditionals; recognizing propaganda devices, bias, and prejudice.
- **Grade 9:** Anglo-American literature; communicative styles (frozen, formal, consultative, casual, intimate); conditionals in expressing arguments; synthesizing linear and non-linear texts.
- **Grade 10:** World literature; evaluating argumentative texts and rhetorical devices; formulating claims of fact, value, and policy; critique writing and research paper methodologies.

### 4.4 Filipino
- **Grade 7:** Panitikang Katutubo at Karunungang-bayan (bugtong, tanaga, sawikain, salawikain, kuwentong-bayan, pabula, alamat, epiko); koridong *Ibong Adarna*; mga pahayag sa pagbibigay ng patunay, sanhi, at bunga.
- **Grade 8:** Panitikang Pilipino sa iba't ibang panahon; awit na *Florante at Laura* ni Francisco Balagtas; antas ng wika, pagpapalawak ng pangungusap, pagsulat ng sanaysay.
- **Grade 9:** Panitikang Asyano (maikling kuwento, tula, dula mula sa Timog-Silangang Asya); nobelang *Noli Me Tangere* ni Dr. Jose Rizal; pagpapahayag ng damdamin at opinyon.
- **Grade 10:** Panitikang Pandaigdig (mitolohiya, parabula, sanaysay mula sa Kanluran at Silangan); nobelang *El Filibusterismo* ni Dr. Jose Rizal; panunuring pampanitikan at maka-Pilipinong pananaliksik.

### 4.5 Araling Panlipunan (AP)
- **Grade 7:** Heograpiya at Sinaunang Kasaysayan ng Pilipinas at Timog-Silangang Asya; kalinangang Austronesyano; unang yugto ng kolonyalismo at imperyalismong Kanluranin; nasyonalismo at pagkabuo ng mga bansa sa rehiyon.
- **Grade 8:** Kasaysayan ng Daigdig; heograpiyang pantao; sinaunang kabihasnan (Mesopotamia, Ehipto, Indus, Mesoamerika); klasikal na Greece at Rome; Gitnang Panahon; Rebolusyong Siyentipiko at Industriyal; Una at Ikalawang Digmaang Pandaigdig.
- **Grade 9:** Ekonomiks; alokasyon, kakapusan, pangangailangan, produksiyon, pagkonsumo; demand, supply, elastisidad, estruktura ng pamilihan; pambansang kita, implasyon, patakarang piskal at pananalapi; sektor ng agrikultura, industriya, at paglilingkod.
- **Grade 10:** Mga Kontemporaryong Isyu; suliraning pangkapaligiran, disaster risk reduction, climate change; globalisasyon, isyu sa paggawa, migrasyon; karapatang pantao, graft and corruption; kasarian at lipunan; aktibong pakikilahok ng mamamayan sa pamamahala.

### 4.6 Values Education / Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (VE / EsP)
- **Grade 7:** Gamit ng isip at kilos-loob sa pagpapasiya; dignidad ng tao; paglinang ng mga birtud (maingat na paghuhusga / prudence, katatagan / fortitude); pamilya bilang sandigan ng pagpapahalaga.
- **Grade 8:** Ang pakikipagkapwa; komunikasyon sa pamilya; pakikipagkaibigan; pamamahala sa emosyon; katapatan sa salita at gawa; seksuwalidad ng tao.
- **Grade 9:** Lipunan at kabutihang panlahat; prinsipyo ng subsidiarity at solidarity; katarungang panlipunan; dignidad ng paggawa; pamamahala sa oras at yamang naipon; pagpili ng kursong akademiko o teknikal-bokasyonal.
- **Grade 10:** Konsensiya at likas na batas moral; tunay na kahulugan ng kalayaan; kasagraduhan ng buhay (anti-abortion, anti-euthanasia, mental health); pagmamahal sa bayan (patriotism); pangangalaga sa kalikasan; paninindigan sa katotohanan.

### 4.7 MAPEH
- **Music & Arts:** Philippine folk and indigenous music; Southeast Asian, East Asian, and Western classical musical traditions; contemporary and modern art movements; digital and media-based arts.
- **Physical Education & Health:** Health-related fitness testing (BMI, push-ups, sit-and-reach, 3-minute step test); target games, invasion games, and team sports (basketball, volleyball, badminton); adolescent mental health, nutrition, disease prevention, and substance abuse prevention (tobacco, alcohol, illegal drugs).

### 4.8 Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE)
- **Grades 7–8 (Exploratory):** Agri-Fishery Arts (farm tools, crop care), Family & Consumer Science (food preparation, garments, handicraft), Industrial Arts (hand tools, basic wiring, carpentry), Information and Communications Technology (ICT hardware, word processing, spreadsheets, typing).
- **Grades 9–10 (Specialized):** Computer Systems Servicing, Food and Beverage Services, Cookery, Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW), Electrical Installation and Maintenance (EIM), Agricultural Crop Production.

## 5. Senior High School Curriculum Structure (Grades 11–12)

Senior High School consists of three subject categories: Core, Applied, and Specialized.

### 5.1 The 15 Core Subjects (Universal across All Tracks)
1. **Oral Communication in Context (Grade 11, Sem 1):** Communication models (Shannon-Weaver, Schramm), speech contexts (interpersonal, public, mass), speech styles, speech acts (Searle), communicative strategies, speech writing, and extemporaneous delivery.
2. **Reading and Writing Skills (Grade 11, Sem 2):** Text as connected discourse, paragraph development patterns, coherence, cohesion, language mechanics, critical reading, assertions, counterclaims, literature review, resume, and application letters.
3. **Komunikasyon at Pananaliksik sa Wika at Kulturang Pilipino (Grade 11, Sem 1):** Mga konseptong pangwika (dayalek, sosyolek, idyolek, register), gamit ng wika sa lipunan (Halliday), kasaysayan ng wikang pambansa, mga sitwasyong pangwika sa media at internet, kakayahang komunikatibo (Dell Hymes).
4. **Pagbasa at Pagsusuri ng Iba't Ibang Teksto Tungo sa Pananaliksik (Grade 11, Sem 2):** Uri ng teksto (impormatibo, deskriptibo, persuweysib, naratibo, argumentatibo), pagsusuri ng datos, at mga hakbang sa maka-Pilipinong pananaliksik.
5. **21st Century Literature from the Philippines and the World (Grade 11/12):** Pre-colonial to contemporary Philippine regional literatures, 21st-century genres (graphic fiction, flash fiction, spoken word), world literary masterpieces, and critical lenses.
6. **Contemporary Philippine Arts from the Regions (Grade 11/12):** Philippine contemporary visual arts, music, dance, theater, architecture, National Artists (*Orden ng Pambansang Alagad ng Sining*), and GAMABA (*Gawad sa Manlilikha ng Bayan*) traditions.
7. **Media and Information Literacy (Grade 11/12):** Traditional vs. new media, information evaluation, digital copyright, fair use, netiquette, combating misinformation/fake news, visual and motion media design principles.
8. **General Mathematics (Grade 11, Sem 1):** Functions and their graphs (rational, inverse, exponential, logarithmic), simple and compound interest, simple and general annuities, stocks, bonds, business/consumer loans, and proposition logic.
9. **Statistics and Probability (Grade 11, Sem 2):** Discrete random variables, normal distribution z-scores, sampling distributions, Central Limit Theorem, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing (z-test, t-test), Pearson r correlation, and linear regression.
10. **Earth and Life Science (Grade 11, Sem 1 for Non-STEM):** Earth systems, minerals, rocks, exogenic and endogenic processes, geologic time, bioenergetics, cellular transport, reproduction, organ systems, and evolutionary ecology.
11. **Physical Science (Grade 11/12 for Non-STEM):** Big Bang nucleosynthesis, stellar evolution, chemical polarity, intermolecular forces, collision theory, renewable energy, Galilean and Newtonian mechanics, wave-particle duality of light, and cosmology.
12. **Introduction to the Philosophy of the Human Person (Grade 11/12):** Philosophical reflection, methods of philosophizing, embodied spirit, environmental ethics, freedom and responsibility, intersubjectivity, and human finitude.
13. **Personal Development / Pansariling Kaunlaran (Grade 11):** Self-awareness, adolescent development, emotional intelligence, mental health coping mechanisms, personal and romantic relationships, family genograms, and career planning.
14. **Understanding Culture, Society, and Politics (Grade 11/12):** Cultural relativism, enculturation, social groups, kinship, state and non-state institutions, social stratification, inequalities, and social change.
15. **Physical Education and Health 1–4 (Grades 11 & 12):** Health-related fitness (HRF), FITT principle, individual/dual/team sports, traditional and contemporary dance, outdoor recreation, aquatic activities, and emergency first aid.

### 5.2 The 7 Applied / Contextualized Track Subjects
1. **English for Academic and Professional Purposes (EAPP):** Academic reading strategies, thesis statements, formal summarizing, critique/reaction paper, concept paper, position paper, and survey reports.
2. **Practical Research 1 (Qualitative Research):** Qualitative designs (phenomenology, ethnography, grounded theory, case study), problem statement, literature review, purposive sampling, interviews, thematic coding, and findings report.
3. **Practical Research 2 (Quantitative Research):** Experimental vs. non-experimental designs, variable operationalization, sampling size formulas, instrument validity and reliability, descriptive and inferential statistics (t-test, ANOVA, Chi-Square).
4. **Filipino sa Piling Larang (Akademik / Tech-Voc):** Pagsulat ng abstrak, sintesis, bionote, panukalang proyekto, katitikan ng pulong, posisyong papel, manwal, at liham-pangnegosyo.
5. **Empowerment Technologies (ETech):** Advanced productivity tools (complex spreadsheets, mail merge), visual and web design, CMS platforms, collaborative tools, and online advocacy campaigns.
6. **Entrepreneurship:** Market identification, 7Ps marketing mix, business models, 4Ms of operations, financial forecasting, cash flow, income statement, and small business enterprise simulation.
7. **Inquiries, Investigations, and Immersion (3Is):** Synthesis of PR1 and PR2 methodologies, culminating research manuscript, formal defense, and knowledge dissemination.

### 5.3 Specialized Academic Strands
- **STEM:**
  - *Pre-Calculus:* Conic sections (circles, parabolas, ellipses, hyperbolas), systems of nonlinear equations, series, sigma notation, mathematical induction, binomial theorem, unit circle, trigonometric identities, polar coordinates.
  - *Basic Calculus:* Limits, continuity, derivative definition, power/product/quotient/chain rules, implicit differentiation, optimization, related rates, antiderivatives, Riemann sums, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, plane area integration.
  - *General Biology 1 & 2:* Cytology, cell cycle, macromolecule biochemistry, cellular energetics, plant and animal organ systems, molecular genetics, evolutionary mechanisms, biodiversity.
  - *General Physics 1 & 2:* 1D/2D kinematics, vectors, Newton's laws, work-energy, momentum, rotational dynamics, electrostatics, Gauss's law, DC circuits, Kirchhoff's rules, magnetism, electromagnetic induction, optics.
  - *General Chemistry 1 & 2:* Stoichiometry, atomic models, periodic trends, chemical bonding, Lewis structures, molecular geometry, intermolecular forces, thermodynamics, reaction kinetics, chemical equilibrium, acid-base buffers.
  - *Disaster Readiness and Risk Reduction (DRRR):* Hazard vulnerability, earthquake, volcano, landslide, hydrometeorological risks, fire safety, and Philippine DRRM protocols (RA 10121).
- **ABM:**
  - *Fundamentals of Accountancy, Business, and Management (FABM 1 & 2):* Complete accounting cycle for service and merchandising firms, balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement, ratio analysis.
  - *Business Mathematics:* Fractions, decimals, percentages, mark-up, mark-down, gross/net margins, payroll computations, deductions, break-even analysis.
  - *Business Finance:* Financial planning, working capital management, time value of money, loan amortization, risk-return trade-offs, capital budgeting.
  - *Organization and Management:* Principles of management, SWOT/PESTEL analysis, organizational design, staffing, leadership theories, control mechanisms.
  - *Applied Economics:* Supply, demand, elasticity, market equilibrium, macroeconomic indicators, competitive analysis.
  - *Business Ethics and Social Responsibility:* Philosophical foundations of ethics, corporate social responsibility (CSR), stakeholder relations, business codes of conduct.
- **HUMSS:**
  - *Philippine Politics and Governance:* Political ideologies, 1987 Constitution, executive, legislative, judicial branches, local government units (LGU), elections, civil society.
  - *Disciplines and Ideas in the Social Sciences (DISS):* Structural-functionalism, Marxism, symbolic interactionism, psychoanalysis, rational choice, Sikolohiyang Pilipino, Pantayong Pananaw.
  - *Disciplines and Ideas in the Applied Social Sciences (DIASS):* Counseling theories, social work interventions, communication professions, code of ethics.
  - *Creative Writing & Creative Nonfiction:* Poetics, figurative language, narrative arc, short fiction, memoirs, personal essays.
  - *Community Engagement, Solidarity, and Citizenship (CESC):* Community profiling, participatory planning, human rights advocacy.
  - *Introduction to World Religions and Belief Systems:* Dharmic, Daoic, and Abrahamic religious traditions.

### 5.4 Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) Specializations
Aligned with TESDA National Certificate (NC) standards offered at TRIS:
- **Computer Systems Servicing (CSS NC II):** PC disassembly/assembly, BIOS/UEFI setup, Windows/Linux OS installation, device drivers, LAN cabling (UTP, RJ-45, T568A/B), router configuration, server deployment (DHCP, DNS, file/print sharing), system maintenance and hardware troubleshooting.
- **Cookery NC II:** Kitchen hygiene (HACCP), knife skills, preparation of appetizers, salads, dressings, stocks, soups, mother sauces, poultry, meat, seafood dishes, and portion-controlled plating.
- **Bread and Pastry Production NC II:** Baking chemistry, yeast dough fermentation, pan de sal, dinner rolls, pies, tarts, choux pastry, sponge/chiffon cakes, buttercream, fondant icing, and petits fours.
- **Food and Beverage Services NC II:** Dining area preparation, napkin folding, table appointments, American/French/Russian table service, guest welcoming, order taking, beverage service, and guest complaints resolution.
- **Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW NC I & II):** Welding safety, welding symbols, 1F, 2F, 3F, 4F fillet welds, and groove welds on carbon steel plates and pipes.
- **Electrical Installation and Maintenance (EIM NC II):** Electrical schematics, EMT conduit bending, wiring of convenience outlets, single-pole and multi-way lighting circuits, and distribution panel circuit breakers.
- **Agricultural Crop Production NC II:** Farm tool maintenance, nursery construction, seed treatment, field planting, fertilizer application, integrated pest management, and post-harvest handling.

## 6. Research Limitations and Unresolved Questions

1. **Phased MATATAG Implementation in Key Stage 3:**  
   DepEd Order No. 010, s. 2024 schedules Grade 9 rollout for SY 2026–2027 and Grade 10 rollout for SY 2027–2028. While official MATATAG curriculum guides and Budget of Work documents for all four grades (7–10) are published on the DepEd website, some secondary schools maintain transitional MELCs-based pacing for Grade 10 until full mandatory nationwide implementation. Katuwang accommodates both by storing curriculum version tags (`MATATAG_2024` vs. `K12_2016`).
2. **School-Specific Electives at TRIS:**  
   Specific TVL course offerings and academic strand electives at Taysan Resettlement Integrated School depend on local teacher credentials, facility accreditation (e.g. computer lab capacity, commercial kitchen workshops), and division allocations. The Katuwang curriculum database is structured to support all official DepEd offerings so that administrators can toggle subjects active or inactive via `/admin/subjects` without schema alterations.
3. **Strengthened Senior High School (SSHS) Pilot Evolution:**  
   The SSHS program is currently in active piloting and policy review. The database preserves established 2016 SHS course codes and titles (recognized nationwide and used in current college entrance tests) while establishing clear mapping for SSHS consolidated domains.
