## 3. Gap Analysis & Resolution Strategy

1. **Junior High School Values Education:**  
   - *Status:* Values Education (Republic Act No. 11476) formally supersedes Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (EsP) under the MATATAG curriculum.
   - *Resolution:* Added `VALUES_EDUCATION` to catalog with display name "Values Education" and comprehensive topics spanning moral discernment, subsidiarity, and human dignity.
2. **Multi-Grade Subject Representation:**  
   - *Status:* Core subjects like `MATH` and `SCIENCE` are taught across all 4 JHS grades (7 to 10), but their topic complexity progresses spirally.
   - *Resolution:* Preserved single stable slug `MATH` and `SCIENCE` with distinct, grade-specific topic tags so that classes with `gradeLevel: GRADE_7` or `gradeLevel: GRADE_10` link to their curriculum-appropriate topics without duplicate subject rows.
3. **Senior High School Subject Differentiation:**  
   - *Status:* SHS subjects are semester-length specialized courses (e.g., General Mathematics vs. Pre-Calculus vs. Business Mathematics).
   - *Resolution:* Modeled as dedicated subject slugs (e.g., `SHS_GENMATH`, `SHS_STEM_PRECALC`, `SHS_ABM_BUSMATH`) with their official DepEd titles, preventing conceptual collision with JHS subjects while integrating directly into Katuwang's dropdowns and matching engine.
